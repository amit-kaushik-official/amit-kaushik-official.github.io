Speaking

Amit Kaushik, Global Head of Growth Marketing at insureMO, Singapore

Amit speaks on growth marketing, performance marketing, go-to-market strategy and SEO and AI search. His full speaker profile is on Sessionize.

How marketing teams can turn performance, brand and lifecycle into one growth engine

Performance marketing, demand generation, brand and lifecycle are often run as separate teams with separate targets. This session looks at how to connect them into one growth system, with shared metrics, clear ownership and tighter links to sales and product.

Getting found in AI search: what marketers should change now

AI overviews and chat assistants now answer many questions before a user clicks a link. This session covers how these tools decide which sources to trust and what marketers can change on their own sites and profiles to be described accurately.

Scaling growth marketing across APAC, Europe and the US

Growing in several regions at once means balancing one global strategy with local channels, language and proof points. This session shares a practical framework for deciding what to centralise, what to localise and how to measure growth across regions.
